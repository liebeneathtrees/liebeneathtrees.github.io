About beneathtrees

beneathtrees is an independent collection of field guides and reference tools for curious people: hobbyists, hikers, travelers, students and anyone who wants to look a little closer at the world around them.

Each guide is built to be practical in the field and pleasant to read at home, with clear photos, careful sources and tools that help you find, identify and record what you see.

  • Free, with no adsEvery guide is free to use, with nothing to buy and nothing selling to you.
  • Private by designNo accounts and no tracking. Anything you save stays in your own browser.
  • Works anywhereMade for phones first, and just as useful on a computer.
